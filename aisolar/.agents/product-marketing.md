## Product Overview
**One-liner:** The operating system for Irish solar installers.
**What it does:** AISOLAR reads the day/night split from a homeowner's electricity
bill and turns it into a credible, bill-based quote. A runtime of ten agents then
runs the back office — survey scheduling, proposal drafting, SEAI grant tracking,
ESB microgen forms, install coordination and follow-ups. The crews install; the
platform does the rest.
**Product category:** Solar installer software / solar CRM + operations platform (Ireland).
**Product type:** Vertical B2B SaaS, per-seat.
**Business model:** Subscription. Solo €197/mo base (€148 yearly), + €97 per extra
seat; tiers Solo → AISolar → AITeam → AIOS (custom). Free trial to start.

**Brand architecture (Cal-confirmed 2026-07-24):** AISolar is the hero you buy.
It has two named *faces*, each the home for a seat on the team — market these with
prominence, but NEVER as new rungs on the offer ladder (the ladder is AIOS →
AISolar / AITeam; the builder does not touch it):
- **AISales** — the closer's home (the consultant cockpit): bill→quote, the "opened
  4×" engagement + bottleneck intelligence, the pipeline.
- **AIField** — the crew's home (the installer portal): jobs, surveys, install, certs.
- **AIChat** — the customer's window (quiet third, customer-facing; not headline).
Spine line: **"A home for every seat — AISales for closers, AIField for crews."**
This threads the AISolar landing, the Agents page, and per-seat pricing. All three
already exist in-app, so marketing them is truth-pass clean.

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** Irish solar installation companies — from solo operators to
multi-crew regional installers, and (later) county franchises.
**Decision-makers:** Owner/MD (buyer), sales consultants (daily users), installers
(field users). Homeowners are the *end-user* of the embeddable calculator, not the buyer.
**Primary use case:** Turn a bill into an honest quote and run the whole
lead→survey→proposal→grant→install→follow-up pipeline without hiring back-office staff.
**Jobs to be done:**
- Turn a customer's actual bill into a credible quote fast (win against 2–3 other quotes).
- Run the back office (scheduling, proposals, SEAI/ESB paperwork, follow-ups) on autopilot.
- Capture and qualify leads from the installer's own website (embeddable calculator).
**Use cases:** bill upload → 21-point estimate; homeowner draws roof on satellite →
sized system; consultant reviews estimate/proposal with engagement + bottleneck
intelligence; agents prepare SEAI application + ESB NC5/6/7 forms for a registered person to sign.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Irish installers drown in back-office admin — scheduling, proposals,
SEAI grant paperwork, ESB microgen forms, follow-ups — and quote off averages instead
of the customer's real bill, so they lose winnable deals.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Manual/spreadsheet back-office doesn't scale and drops follow-ups.
- Generic CRMs aren't solar- or Ireland-aware (no SEAI math, no ESB forms, no bill read).
- Generic online calculators use average homes, so the number doesn't survive scrutiny.
**What it costs them:** lost deals to faster competitors, hours of admin per job, and
grant/compliance errors.
**Emotional tension:** the owner feels the business can't grow without hiring admin; the
consultant feels they're a paperwork clerk, not a closer.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Generic installer CRMs / solar quoting tools — fall short: not Ireland-specific
(no SEAI grant tiers, no ESB NC forms), quote off averages not the bill.
**Secondary:** Spreadsheets + email + a shared calendar — falls short: no automation,
follow-ups slip, no compliance pack.
**Indirect:** Hiring more office/admin staff — falls short: fixed cost that scales
linearly and still quotes off averages.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- **Bill-based, not averages** — reads the day/night split + up to 21 points from the bill.
- **Ireland-native compliance** — SEAI grant math (€700/€200, cap €1,800, 0% VAT) and ESB
  NC5/6/7 forms built in.
- **Ten agents run the office** — prepare + track; a registered person signs and sends (draft-first).
- **Embeddable tenant-branded calculator** — a lead engine the installer drops on their own site.
- **Consultant intelligence** — engagement signal ("opened 4×") + stage-derived bottleneck + next action.
- **A home for every seat** — AISales for closers, AIField for crews (see brand architecture).
**Why customers choose us:** it's the only tool that does the *Irish* solar back office and
grounds every quote in the customer's own bill.

## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "Another CRM/tool to learn." | It's Ireland-solar-specific and does the SEAI/ESB work generic tools can't — the bill read and the pack are the point. |
| "Will it replace my people?" | No. Agents prepare and track; your registered people sign, and your crews install. Draft-first, nothing auto-sends. |

**Anti-persona:** non-Irish installers (grants/ESB are Ireland-specific); homeowners
wanting to DIY (the platform is for installers, though the calculator is for their customers).

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** admin overload, slipped follow-ups, SEAI/ESB paperwork pain, quotes that lose.
**Pull:** bill-based quotes in minutes, agents running the office, an embeddable calculator that brings leads.
**Habit:** spreadsheets + an existing generic CRM + manual SEAI forms.
**Anxiety:** migration effort and trusting automation — answered by draft-first (human signs/sends) and bill-grounded, survey-confirmed numbers.
